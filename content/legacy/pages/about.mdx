# About SkyShield Technology

SkyShield Technology is a Moroccan deep-tech cybersecurity company built to combine elite offensive and defensive expertise with AI-driven automation and productized security delivery.

## Company Background

Founded in Agadir by cybersecurity engineer Youssef Aghayou, SkyShield started by solving practical security gaps for startups and growing organizations, then expanded into enterprise-grade assessments, monitoring, incident response, and advisory programs.

## Technology Stack & Architecture

- Security testing workflows for web, API, mobile, cloud, and network environments

- Automated collection, normalization, and triage pipelines for security signals

- Layered architecture: assessment, detection, response, governance, and reporting

- Scalable service modules aligned to ISO 27001 and NIST control families

## AI Automation Approach

- AI-assisted analysis to reduce manual-only bottlenecks in operations

- Prioritization models for faster remediation and clearer executive reporting

- Human-in-the-loop validation for accuracy, safety, and business context

- Continuous tuning based on incident outcomes and evolving threats

## Sponsors & Partners

- SkyShield collaborates with incubator programs, cloud ecosystem partners, and cybersecurity communities to accelerate practical deployment, local talent development, and trusted market delivery.

- 1000 Fikra - Afriquia

## Vision & Long-Term Roadmap

- Build a leading AI-powered cybersecurity company from Morocco

- Launch productized autonomous defense modules for SMEs and enterprises

- Expand regional coverage while exporting trusted services internationally

- Invest in research, attack simulation labs, and training pipelines

## Leadership

SkyShield is led by founder and cybersecurity engineer Youssef Aghayou, focused on delivery quality, measurable security outcomes, and scalable service architecture for long-term client resilience.
